Juxta Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Overview

Juxta is a web browser for iPhone made by Osmar Hernandez-Bojorges. This policy describes what the app does with information on your device.

Information stored on your iPhone

Bookmarks you save, browsing history for pages you open outside private browsing (up to 500 visits), open tab addresses, and settings such as Tri-Screen mode, full screen, appearance, search engine, new tab page, and Clear All Data on Exit stay on this iPhone. Juxta does not send that information to the developer. Deleting the app removes it. Clear All Data on Exit, and Clear all browsing data now in Settings, remove history and website data.

Information the developer does not collect

Juxta has no account, no advertising, and no analytics. The developer does not receive your browsing history, bookmarks, searches, location, contacts, or photos, and does not sell or share personal information.

Websites you visit

When you open a page, your device connects directly to that website. What the site does with your request is covered by that site's own policy. Pages are loaded with the system WebKit engine.

Search

If you type words into the address bar or the new tab page instead of a web address, Juxta opens a search for those words. DuckDuckGo is the default. You can switch to Google in Settings. The query is sent to that search engine as a normal page request. Juxta does not receive a copy of it. The choice stays on this iPhone.

Passkeys

Allow Passkey Sign-In in Settings asks iOS for permission to use a passkey already saved on this iPhone when a website offers that sign-in. The passkey stays with iOS. Juxta does not receive it.

Camera, microphone, and location

A website can ask to use the camera, microphone, or your location. iOS shows a permission prompt first. If you allow it, only that website can use it, and only while you are using Juxta. You can change these permissions later in the iOS Settings app. Juxta does not access the camera, microphone, or location for itself.

Private browsing

Private browsing uses a temporary cookie store for that screen and does not add pages to History. Leaving private browsing discards those cookies. It does not make you anonymous to the websites you open.

Children

Juxta is a general-purpose browser with unrestricted access to the web. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted at this address and shown in the app under Settings, Privacy Policy.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support page linked from the App Store listing for Juxta.
