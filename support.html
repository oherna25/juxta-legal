Juxta Support

Juxta is a web browser. There is no account to sign in to.

One screen or more than one

Open the display menu at the bottom left and choose Cover Screen or Inner Screen. On the inner screen, the Dual and Tri switcher in the top bar shows one page or two side by side. Settings has the same choice as Tri-Screen mode.

Full screen

Full Screen in a screen's controls lets the page reach the edges of that screen. Each screen remembers its own choice. Scrolling down a page hides the top bar, and scrolling up brings it back.

Appearance

Settings, under Display, has Appearance. System follows the iPhone. Light and Dark stay put. Dark mode also asks websites for their dark theme.

Default browser

Settings has Set as Default Browser. That opens the iOS screen where you choose which app opens links. Pick Juxta there.

Private browsing and clearing data

The hand icon on a screen opens a private session for that screen only. It is left out of History. Settings has Clear All Data on Exit and Clear all browsing data now.
